<b>beTime</b> is a minimalist, open-source productivity time tracker designed for clean, distraction-free activity logging.

<b>Core Features</b>
- <b>Maximum Tracking Flexibility:</b> Whether you use Pomodoro sessions or open-ended timers, customize labels, reorderable widgets, and stats to fit any workflow.
- <b>Dynamic Minimalist Design:</b> A clean, responsive Material 3 interface that adapts smoothly to your routines, actions, and light or dark themes.

<b>Private by Design</b>
- No accounts or cloud sync required
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
- All your time logs and labels stay locally on your device
